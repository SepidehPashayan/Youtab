About Youtab

We don't build cars. We build the feeling of driving one.

Youtab is an Iranian idea for the future of car making — where the raw power of an American muscle car meets the engineering precision of a European sport sedan. Every Youtab model is designed around two feelings: the push into your seat under acceleration, and total confidence through a corner. We're still at the concept stage, but every detail on this page is part of a vision meant to become real.
